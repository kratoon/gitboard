- **Question:** `getBoard` and `getBacklog` return every card with its full Markdown body, checklist and hash (`boardFor` maps each card through `toIssue`). Should board and backlog responses carry summaries and leave bodies to per-issue fetches?
- **Why it matters:** the response is O(total body bytes): about 1.5 MB per 1,000 issues at an assumed 1.5 KB each, 15 MB at 10,000. Harmless on loopback, costly over a LAN or WAN to many users. It is an API change, not a storage one.
- **Recommendation:** add a summary projection (title, status, priority, labels, parent, rank, checklist counts, hash) and keep `issue.get` for bodies; keep the parity tests.
