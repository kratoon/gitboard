- **What:** an optional endpoint with a per-repository secret that triggers the same fetch as a poll when the instance is reachable by the forge. Verify the signature (HMAC) before parsing, answer quickly, treat the payload as a hint only.
- **Why deferred:** a NAS is usually unreachable by webhooks; adaptive polling is the baseline and the system is correct without it.
