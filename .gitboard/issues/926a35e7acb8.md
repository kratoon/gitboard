- **Question:** What isolation do we promise between users of the same instance, and against hostile repository content?
- **Why it matters:** It sets the threat model and the cost of the hardening work.
- **Options:** Semi-trusted users (family, friends, a team) with safe defaults; per-user OS accounts or containers; per-tenant virtual machines.
- **Recommendation:** Semi-trusted users, documented plainly. Safe defaults regardless: separate per-user clones and credentials, cone sparse checkout of `.gitboard/`, hooks disabled, no submodules or filters, `transfer.fsckObjects`, size and time limits, a local-path and SSRF policy for repository URLs. Stronger isolation is out of scope; recommend a second instance instead.
